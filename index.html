Yes. Workflows can be sequential, parallel, committee or quorum-based, conditional by amount or other rules, automatically approved, escalated, or delegated. Published workflow versions remain attached to the requests that used them, so later changes do not rewrite history.

Approvers work from a focused decision queue and can also receive time-limited links through email or enabled messaging channels. They can approve, approve with conditions, reject, return, request information, delegate, or escalate when the workflow permits.

ApprovalDesk adds every decision, revision, reminder, delegation, and workflow event as a new history entry. Recorded actions cannot be silently edited or removed. Authorised administrators and auditors can also create verifiable evidence packs for individual requests.
